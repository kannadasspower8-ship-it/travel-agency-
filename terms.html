Terms & Conditions & Booking Policy

Please review these terms governing travel bookings with Thirumala Travel Agency.

1. Booking & Confirmation

A booking is considered confirmed once the initial reservation advance (typically 30% to 50% depending on hotel category and peak season) is received. Upon confirmation, a verified booking voucher listing all hotel stays, itinerary days, and vehicle assignments is issued.

2. Inter-Island Ferry Tickets

Private catamarans (Makruzz, Nautika, Green Ocean) issue passenger tickets based on government identity proof. It is mandatory for travelers to provide accurate full names, age, and valid ID numbers as matching the government photo ID carried during travel.

3. Weather Disruptions & Force Majeure

Andaman experiences tropical weather. In the rare event of severe sea depression or Coast Guard warnings where ferry sailings between Port Blair, Havelock, or Neil are suspended for public safety, Thirumala Travel Agency's ground team will arrange alternate mainland sightseeing or assist with rescheduling. Extra accommodation or meal charges arising from force majeure are handled in accordance with hotel operator policies.

4. Island Rules & Environmental Laws

  • Collection or removal of sea corals, live shells, or marine specimens is strictly prohibited under the Wildlife Protection Act.
  • Single-use plastic bottles and plastic bags are banned in various ecological zones across Andaman.
  • Photography, video recording, or any form of interaction in the Jarawa Tribal Reserve corridor is a cognizable legal offense.
