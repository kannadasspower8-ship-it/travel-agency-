Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear and transparent guidelines for booking cancellations and refund processing.

At Thirumala Travel Agency, we understand that travel plans can change unexpectedly. We strive to keep our cancellation policy as flexible and customer-friendly as possible, subject to our contracted partner resort and ferry operator terms.

Standard Package Cancellation Slabs

Notice Period Before Travel Date Refund Percentage
30 Days or More Prior to Arrival 85% Refund (or 100% Date Reschedule Credit)
15 to 29 Days Prior to Arrival 50% Refund
7 to 14 Days Prior to Arrival 25% Refund
Less than 7 Days or No Show Non-Refundable (Hotel Peak Retention)

Private Ferry Ticket Cancellations

Ferry tickets (Makruzz, Nautika, Green Ocean) follow the respective marine operator's tariff regulations. Any refund received from the ferry operator upon ticket cancellation is directly credited back to the customer.

Refund Processing Timeline

Eligible refunds are processed via the original payment method (NEFT, UPI, Card) within 5 to 7 working days from the formal date of written cancellation confirmation.
