Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

At Thirumala Travel Agency, located in Port Blair, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, protecting your personal information is paramount. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect when you explore our website or book holiday packages, and how that information is utilized.

1. Information We Collect

When inquiring or making reservations for Andaman holiday packages, ferry tickets (Makruzz/Nautika), hotel stays, or water sports, we may collect:

  • Contact details including full name, phone/WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Government identification details (Aadhaar, Passport) strictly required by Andaman Port Management Board and Directorate of Shipping Services for inter-island ferry ticketing.
  • Travel preferences, tentative dates, guest counts, and meal requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used solely to:

  • Process confirmed hotel vouchers and ferry tickets on your behalf.
  • Coordinate on-ground airport and jetty pickups with our dedicated island drivers.
  • Communicate vital travel advisories, ferry timing updates, and weather notices.

3. Security of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party telemarketers. All customer records are stored securely with restricted access.
