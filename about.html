More Than a Trip. A Journey to Remember.

We are a team of passionate islanders dedicated to showing you the authentic warmth, hidden beaches, and majestic underwater coral beauty of the Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

Our Island Story

Born in Port Blair, Rooted in Island Hospitality

Established over a decade ago, Thirumala Travel Agency was founded and guided by its owner, Thirumalai, with a singular purpose: to deliver transparent, reliable, and deeply memorable travel experiences without the impersonal middleman markups that often plague tour bookings.

When planning a multi-island vacation across Port Blair, Havelock Island, and Neil Island, coordinating catamaran tickets, jetty baggage transfers, verified hotels, and experienced drivers requires precise local knowledge. Our team lives on these islands year-round. When tropical storms cause temporary ferry rescheduling, our coordinators are right on the jetty resolving issues in real time.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide stress-free, deeply authentic island holidays with verified accommodations, crystal-clear pricing, and round-the-clock ground assistance.

👁️ Our Vision

To be the most trusted and environmentally conscious travel company in the Andaman & Nicobar Islands, preserving its coral reef treasures for generations.
